# Hospital Web Application

A comprehensive hospital management web application designed to facilitate and improve healthcare services for patients, staff, and administrators. The application aims to provide affordable and quality healthcare solutions, streamline hospital operations, and enhance patient experience, particularly serving the region of Bijnor and nearby areas.

## Mission

Our mission is to provide affordable and quality healthcare services to the people of Bijnor and surrounding regions, utilizing advanced medical technology and clinical expertise. We strive for excellence in clinical and critical care management, with a vision to develop this institute into an advanced medical college.

## Features

- **Patient Management:** Streamlined appointment booking and patient information system.
- **Doctor Directory:** Detailed profiles of specialist doctors including Orthopedic, Critical Care, and more.
- **Hospital Services:** 
  - Emergency Services (24/7)
  - Intensive Care Unit (ICU)
  - Neonatal ICU (NICU)
  - Laboratory Services
  - Recovery Room
  - Pharmacy (24/7)
  - Bed Facilities
  - Free Checkups
  - Total Care Model
  - Dental Care, Message Therapy, Cardiology, Diagnosis

- **Online Appointment Booking:** Patients can easily book appointments online.
- **User Reviews:** Client testimonials and feedback sections.
- **About & Vision:** Dedicated pages for hospital mission, vision, and background.
